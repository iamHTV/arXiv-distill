# 2610.06069v1 Attention Tax, Handoff Tax: A Stylised Model of When Multi-Agent LLM Systems Help

## Problem
- Literature về multi-agent LLM systems đi đến kết luận trái ngược nhau: phe hoài nghi (Tran & Kiela — data processing inequality (bất đẳng thức xử lý dữ liệu); Ao et al. — decision-theoretic dominance (ưu thế người ra quyết định tập trung)) chứng minh single agent với cùng information + compute phải dominate delegated system; phe lạc quan (Tang et al.) chứng minh multi-agent gain tăng theo task depth; Meyerson et al. giải million-step task với zero errors bằng decomposition; Jwalapuram et al. đặt cả hai trong 1 bảng: self-consistency beats 6 automated multi-agent frameworks hầu hết mọi nơi (thường <1/10 cost) NHƯNG expert-designed decomposition nâng GPT-5 từ 57.0 lên 96.5 trên context-heavy task. Mâu thuẫn đến từ modelling khác bottleneck: mỗi model cố định 1 số trong 5 quantities và để các quantity khác chạy — quantity phe này cố định đúng là quantity phe kia thay đổi. Chưa có model nào có đủ 5 quantities (attention tax (thuế chú ý — cost của context load), handoff tax (thuế bàn giao — information loss khi nén state thành summary), redundancy dependence (phụ thuộc thất bại giữa các samples), verification, budget) để predict WHERE winner flips.

## Contribution
- Stylised reliability model: task = chain L steps; 1 step = 1 LLM call với failure probability ε(c, b) phụ thuộc context load c và thinking budget b; log-loss units g(c,b) = −log(1−ε). 2 structural primitives: decomposition (fresh context cho mỗi agent, giá = handoff loss h) và redundancy (N parallel calls, failures phụ thuộc vì share base model). Assumptions 1–4: product form (steps fail independently given (c,b)), monotone curve (ε nondecreasing in c, nonincreasing in b), context growth (single agent c_t = c0 + kt; decomposed agent thứ i trong chunk thấy c0 + s + ki, s = summary length), handoff monotonicity (h ≥ 0, nonincreasing in s).
- 2 crossover conditions: (a) Decomposition thắng khi attention cost avoided by resetting context vượt handoff cost — Theorem 1 (single-step agents: unique L* tồn tại iff a∞ > heff, với a_t = attention tax tại step t, heff = h + ∆o), Theorem 2 (arbitrary chunk sizes: some m beats single agent for all large L iff A∞ > h — total avoidable tax), Theorem 3 (linear regime closed form: L* = max{2, ⌊2(h+λs)/(λk)⌋+1}), Theorem 4 (chunk size square-root law: m* = √(2h/(λ(k−2s/L)))), Proposition 1 (knee-shaped curves), Proposition 2 (summary length optimum: −h′(s*) = λ); (b) Parallel sampling at equal budget thắng khi shared-failure floor nằm dưới thinking floor — Theorem 5 (majority vote under exchangeable failures → shared-failure mass F = µ(p>1/2) + ½µ(p=1/2)), Theorem 6 (parallel wins for all large N iff F < ε∞(c); sufficient condition chỉ cần (ε_b, ρ): F_max = ρε(1−ε)/(ρε(1−ε)+(½−ε)²) < ε∞(c)), Corollary 3 (bound trên F), Corollary 4 (million-step regime: L=10⁶ cần per-step vote failure và handoff tax đều ~10⁻⁶ → phải pass full explicit state, không phải summary).
- Recovery prior results as restrictions: Tran & Kiela = flat curve (Corollary 1 — sceptics' conclusion là flat corner: single agent weakly dominates mọi decomposition ở mọi depth); Ao et al. = flat curve + capacity-free centre (analogical); Tang et al. = ρ=0 redundancy + free compute (structural); Meyerson et al. = m=1 + per-step vote + full state passed (Corollary 4, empirical); Xu et al. = task noise as h + model noise as curve (analogical).
- 2 measurements thật: (a) padded-context pilot trên gpt-oss-20b (1,000 calls, 5 loads); (b) ledger-reconciliation experiment (40,600 calls, 309M prompt tokens, ~$10, 27 phút) — đo in-task curve + handoff tax từ single-agent và handoff runs alone, predict crossover ở depth 10 và decomposed-system success trong vòng 9 percentage points ở depths 20/50/100.

## Method
- Handoff tax (Definition 1) đo operational: h = G_M − G_H + Σ[g(c0+|H|+ki, b) − g(c0+s+ki, b)] — chạy cùng chunk từ summary M và từ full workspace H, lấy curve term cộng lại; measurable, không counterfactual-only.
- Pilot: gpt-oss-20b (Ollama, 20.9B params, MXFP4, native context 131,072), Ollama 0.30.8, 1× RTX 3090, num_ctx 71,680, flash attention, 8-bit KV cache, reasoning level low, temperature 0.7, output budget 1,024 tokens. Task: chained arithmetic word problems (6 operations, exact integer answer), 100 problems × 2 samples, context padding nominal 0/2k/8k/32k/64k (measured 107/2,117/8,023/31,641/63,129 tokens).
- Ledger experiment: ledger L transactions, exact integer running balance từ 50,000; 6 kinds (deposit, withdrawal, percentage fee rounded down, hold, release trả hold 3–12 steps sau tên bằng id, reversal undo 3–12 steps trước tên bằng id — 2 kinds cuối = long-range dependencies); mỗi block pad ~400 tokens (measured 398 tokens/step); 100 ledgers giống nhau mỗi depth, paired comparison. Single agent: 1 growing conversation, c0 = 197 tokens. Decomposed: m = 5 consecutive transactions per fresh agent; handoff note ~300 tokens (225 words) carrying balance, mọi open hold, id+amount của 12 transactions cuối. Handoff tax đo trên 100 ledgers riêng bằng paired design (12 transactions continue from history, 5 tiếp theo từ note, note allowances 100/300/900 tokens → produced notes 137/183/255 tokens). gpt-oss-20b trên hosted OpenAI-compatible endpoint, reasoning low, temp 0.7, 512-token allowance/call (b) cho cả 2 systems; handoff notes là separate calls 600-token allowance (∆o = 0). Grading: per-step increment failure (stationary = exclude fee steps), final balance exact; McNemar paired tests.

## Key Findings
- Pilot — 2 curves từ 1,000 calls: arithmetic failures FLAT: 5.5/5.0/5.5/3.5/6.0% (paired 64k-vs-rest +1.1pp, SE 1.9, t99 = 0.59; slope CI [−6.1,+6.6]×10⁻⁷/token — flat within 4 points over 63k tokens). Format failures (thiếu ANSWER: line): 0/0.5/5.0/6.5/25.5% (+22.5pp, SE 3.4, t99 = 6.7; 47/51 still có đúng number — model ngừng nói nó dưới dạng yêu cầu, strict parser downstream fail cả 51). Strict: 5.5/5.5/10.0/10.0/29.5%. Hinge fit: knee ~27k tokens, post-knee slope 7.8×10⁻⁶/token [3.4, 11.1]×10⁻⁶. → curve là property của model + task + failure definition cùng lúc.
- Ledger in-task curve (17,000 single-agent steps): failure 2.7% (<2k) → 71% (>36k) — steep hơn pilot nhiều vì mọi step phụ thuộc accumulated trajectory. Stationary kinds: 2.1/4.9/6.6/12.9/22.7/54.8/68.2% theo load bins. Reasoning grew với load (20 tokens/step <4k → 153 >30k); 5.1% single-agent calls hit 512-token budget vs 0.13% decomposed. Fee steps đo state carried in chứ không phải step: tại constant load fee failure 7%→72% sau transaction 50; conditioned on correct preceding balance flat (5/91 early, 3/55 late; 283/340 late fees sau wrong balance fail).
- Handoff tax: continue from history (mean load 6.2k): post-boundary stationary steps fail 5.2%; start from note (mean load 1.6k): 3.2/2.0/1.8% cho 3 note lengths. Raw comparison favours note (history ngồi cao hơn trên curve); Definition 1 cộng curve term lại (0.172 nats/chunk) → increment tax h_inc = 0.077/0.026/0.015 nats cho notes 137/183/255 tokens. Balance preservation: 100/99/100 trên 100 calibration ledgers; h_bal = −log(0.99) = 0.010; total taxes 0.077/0.036/0.015 nats, nonincreasing in note length. Bootstrap 95% CI: increment tax [−0.11, 0.16], total [−0.10, 0.18] — negative projections set về 0 theo Assumption 4.
- Prediction & result: crossover L* = 10 (bootstrap quantiles 6 và 27). Predicted vs observed (single/decomposed): L=20: stationary predicted 0.46/0.61, observed 0.50/0.69, final 0.49/0.64, McNemar p = 0.014/0.049; L=50: predicted 0.01/0.27, observed stationary 0.06/0.24, final 0.02/0.18, p = 0.001/0.003; L=100: predicted 0.00/0.07, observed stationary 0.00/0.11, final 0.00/0.10, p <0.001/0.002. Decomposition won at every tested depth, both gradings; paired improvements 0.19/0.18/0.11, bootstrap CIs [0.05,0.33]/[0.08,0.28]/[0.05,0.18]. Predictions miss observations ≤9 percentage points (single agent: +4/+5/0 points trên prediction — direction product form imply khi failures cluster trên hard ledgers; decomposed: +8.4/−3.1/+3.9, no consistent sign). Sensitivity với h_joint = 0.067: decomposed predictions 0.55/0.20/0.04, vẫn ahead mọi depth. No-truncation subset (98/62/12 pairs): final-balance 0.49 vs 0.65, 0.02 vs 0.16, 0 vs 0.08.
- Token usage: single agent 806/2,873/10,046 tokens/ledger ở depths 20/50/100 vs decomposed 1,611/4,797/10,195 (incl. notes; 770/2,001/4,078 cho step calls); depth 100: 2 systems generate bằng nhau; input side: single agent xử lý 2.0M prompt tokens/ledger ở depth 100 vs 0.2M decomposed (re-read growing context every step).
- Chunk size: primary h = 0.036 nats + low-load slope ~8×10⁻⁶/token → Theorem 4 approximation √(2h/(λk)) ≈ 4.8 steps — gần m = 5 đã chọn trước khi đo.
- Regime placement: flat region (small k, short contexts) = multi-hop QA, static reasoning benchmarks (Jwalapuram: self-consistency ahead of 6 automated frameworks trên GPQA-Diamond/HLE-Maths, <1/10 cost; redundancy vẫn giúp qua Theorem 5). Rising region (large k hoặc large c0, decomposable task) = SMFR task: expert decomposition GPT-5 57.0→96.5 ($554.82 vs $478.40), GPT-OSS-120B 26.1→36.1; automated frameworks gain ≤6.6 points tại 2–5× cost (pay orchestration overhead mà không buy attention-tax reduction). Competency floor: GPT-4o 22.1→22.6 dưới expert decomposition = nothing — nếu ε ở minimal context đã lớn, decomposition remove attention tax nhưng fresh agents' product vẫn gần zero.
- Redundancy threshold (Theorem 6, worked example): ε(b) = 0.08 + 0.22e^(−b) tại b=1 → threshold correlation ρ* = 0.497; ρ ∈ {0, 0.2} favour parallel sampling, ρ ∈ {0.5, 0.8} favour sequential thinking tại mọi N ∈ {3, 5, 11, 51, 201}. Ledger illustration: ε = 5.5%, ρ = 0.12 → common-shock floor 0.7%, bound 3.1% — parallel sampling beats longer thinking for large N khi thinking floor trên 3.1%.

## Actionable Insights
- Đo trước khi orchestrate: chạy single agent ở increasing context loads trên CHÍNH task của bạn, fit failure-vs-load curve; đo handoff tax bằng paired continue-vs-handoff runs; tính crossover L* = max{2, ⌊2(h+λs)/(λk)⌋+1} (linear regime). Task nằm dưới L* → dùng 1 agent; multi-agent orchestration là pure loss. Ví dụ: doc QA với per-step context growth nhỏ → single agent; tool-heavy chains với k≈400+ tokens/step → decompose past depth ~10.
- Chunk size theo square-root law: mỗi subagent nên own ≈ √(2h/(λk)) steps (overhead cộng vào handoff tax). Handoff rẻ + curve steep → 1 step/agent; handoff đắt hoặc curve flat → 1 agent cho tất cả. Ví dụ: h = 0.036 nats, slope 8×10⁻⁶/token, k = 400 → ~4.8 steps/agent.
- Failure definition là 1 phần của curve: cùng 1,000 calls cho curve flat (arithmetic) và steep (format compliance) — decay of compliance với instruction ngồi xa điểm dùng, không phải reasoning collapse. Lặp format instruction cạnh question để flatten strict curve; paper showing single-agent win short-context + multi-agent win long-context có thể đang đo instruction survival chứ không phải capability.
- Parallel vs sequential at equal budget: majority vote thắng for large N iff shared-failure mass F < thinking floor ε∞(c); check đủ với (ε_b, ρ): F_max < ε∞. Heterogeneous ensembles flip nhiều tasks sang parallel; homogeneous self-consistency saturates. Ví dụ: agent ensemble投票 — measure pairwise failure correlation ρ giữa các samples trước khi tin voting gain.

## Assumptions & Limitations
- Điều kiện: product form (steps fail independently given (c,b)) — ignores within-trajectory dependence, overstates single agent's loss khi failures cluster trên hard instances (hướng thấy trên ledger); monotone curve; handoff tax 1 scalar/boundary (related to Ao et al.'s information-theoretic loss chỉ by analogy); budget fixed per step; redundancy floor phụ thuộc toàn bộ mixing law — measured (ε, ρ) bound chứ không pin floor; vote modelled trên correctness indicator (pessimistic cho open-ended answers với nhiều wrong values).
- Limitation paper thừa nhận: measurements cover 1 model (gpt-oss-20b) trên 2 tasks; cần tiếp: boundary verifier recall, thinking floor, curves cho further models; pilot đo padded session context, không phải distribution real trajectory accumulates; 2 sections' curves không quantitatively comparable (different serving stacks); Proposition 3 conditional on untested hypothesis (fresh-context verifier ít dependent hơn self-check — cần same verifier prompt ở cả 2 positions); runs at depths 2 bên crossover là measurement tiếp theo (crossover depth 10 nằm dưới tested range).
- Limitation CHƯA nói: linear-regime closed forms chỉ local fit (đoán curve shape trên range loads trajectory visits); knee location poorly determined (loosely determined, robust feature chỉ là rise 10%→30% từ 32k→63k); temperature 0.7 + fixed budget có thể không đại diện production serving; handoff note content (balance + holds + 12 transactions) là design choice — tax thay đổi với note schema; không đo verifier recall thực tế (Proposition 3 vẫn conditional).
