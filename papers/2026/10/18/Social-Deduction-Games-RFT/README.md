# 2610.04261v1 Playing social deduction games with reinforcement fine-tuned large language models

## Problem
- RFT (reinforcement fine-tuning — tinh chỉnh tăng cường) ngày càng dùng trong applications mà LLM tương tác với humans và agents khác, nhưng chưa rõ RFT shape social reasoning của LLM agents như thế nào. Prior work mostly EVALUATE model+prompt như object tĩnh; social competence thực ra là tập hợp behaviourally grounded functions. Core khó: terminal win-loss outcome là sparse, noisy signal cho socially interactive learning — 1 win/loss là final consequence của long causal chain (hidden-role inference, public communication, voting, role-specific actions, mistakes của agents khác, stochastic events), cùng 1 outcome có thể từ very different behaviours, 1 useful action có thể nằm trong losing trajectory. Outcome-only RFT nén các causes thành 1 sparse signal → multi-agent credit assignment problem rất khó.

## Contribution
- AgentEvolver Game Arena: multi-agent environment với hidden roles, private observations, public discussion, consequential group decisions; turn-based prompt-chaining protocol; trajectory τ = {(z_i, x_{i,t}, y_{i,t}, a_{i,t}, s_t)}; controlled-policy setting (subset C trainable, rest fixed opponents) + same-side variant (trainable policy trên TẤT CẢ roles của 1 side, shared params, role-conditioned updates — 1 game cho nhiều training trajectories).
- Phân tách social competence thành 2 components đo được: social reading (suy luận latent social state từ dialogue/partial observations — reward so belief state với hidden state: F1 cho set-valued, exact-match cho categorical) và social influence (thay đổi beliefs/decisions của others qua communication — reward đo intended social target vs actions của others sau đó). 3 macro-families games: Elimination (Werewolf/Mafia, One Night Werewolf), Institution (Avalon, Secret Hitler), Asymmetry (Spyfall, Deception: Murder in Hong Kong).
- Reward decomposition cho joint multi-agent social-cognitive RFT: R(τ) = λ_read·R_read + λ_inf·R_inf + λ_aux·R_aux − λ_fmt·P_fmt − λ_leak·P_leak (P_fmt penalize invalid structured outputs, P_leak penalize illegal public disclosure of hidden info); terminal win rate logged nhưng KHÔNG phải primary online reward — dùng làm held-out diagnostic. Tối ưu bằng GRPO (group-relative advantage, clipped policy-gradient + KL penalty, no critic).
- Bằng chứng RFT thay đổi social behaviour theo reward-dependent way, với human evaluation blinded (178 ratings, 5-point scale) và cross-model generalization (9 opponents, 100 games mỗi đối thủ).

## Method
- Models: Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct, Qwen3-4B-Instruct, GLM-4-9B-Chat, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, DeepSeek-7B (replication). Mixed-role setup: focal player sampled across roles (innocent, adversarial, special-information) tránh overfitting fixed perspective.
- Cross-game outcome-only RFT: reward 1 nếu focal side thắng, 0 nếu không.
- Social reading experiments: (1) hidden-state inference — Avalon predict team alignment, Werewolf predict wolf set từ partial transcript; (2) dynamic belief updating — Avalon group by mission, Werewolf focal villager belief by successive days; (3) role-specialized — Avalon Assassin fixed-role variant, outcome-based reward, so sánh Merlin/Servant/Minion.
- Social influence experiments: (1) vote steering — One Night Werewolf Minion, self-selection variant (Minion tự chọn target, rewarded nếu votes của others concentrate vào target đó mà tránh Werewolf); (2) team approval — Avalon: focal agent tạo 1 public response trước approval vote; others vote baseline (không có response) rồi vote lại (có response); reward = post-response outcome đúng hướng target + change in others' votes.
- Multi-agent social-cognitive RFT: prompt-level ablations (no scaffold / SR only / SI only / SR+SI, Werewolf good-side, win rate metric, weights fixed) + full-game RFT (6-player Werewolf, 9-player Werewolf, Avalon) với online reward nhấn intermediate social-cognitive signals; cross-model eval (learned 9-player good-side policy vs 9 wolf-side opponents incl. Qwen3.7-Max, Qwen-Plus, Qwen-Max, Kimi-K2.5, 100 valid games mỗi opponent); human eval (blinded complete 9-player games, 74 matched base-RFT pairs, ratings 1–5 trên 5 dimensions: overall value, social-reading quality, social-influence & coordination, human-like gameplay, strategic correctness; evaluators instructed NOT to high-score merely because good side won).

## Key Findings
- Outcome-only RFT unstable: Secret Hitler mạnh nhất (final ~0.63, avg ~0.61); Avalon khó (0.23→0.39, avg 0.25); Werewolf/Mafia peak 0.88 nhưng end 0.38; One Night Werewolf avg 0.52 non-monotonic; Spyfall flat (avg 0.40, final 0.50); Deception peak 0.75, final 0.50, avg 0.48. → directly optimizing terminal win-loss KHÔNG reliably produce general social-deduction ability.
- Social reading (hidden-state inference) cải thiện rõ ràng cross-model: Avalon Qwen2.5-7B 0.55→0.78, Qwen2.5-14B 0.65→0.71, Qwen3-4B 0.62→0.70, GLM-4-9B 0.58→0.69; Werewolf/Mafia Qwen2.5-7B 0.46→0.65, Qwen2.5-14B 0.47→0.65, Qwen3-4B 0.33→0.44, GLM-4-9B 0.29→0.50, DeepSeek-7B 0.29→0.38.
- Dynamic belief updating (Qwen2.5-7B): Avalon M1 0.58→0.71, M2 0.48→0.85, M3 0.41→0.80 — later missions gain nhiều nhất (mission index không nằm trong target label; evidence tích lũy nhiều hơn). Werewolf: Day-1 0.42→0.53, Day-2 0.38→0.68, Day-3 0.34→0.75 — largest gains ở later game states. Policy tích hợp temporally accumulated evidence, không phải chỉ emit đúng format.
- Role-specialized: Assassin là role DUY NHẤT cải thiện rõ sustained; response length GIẢM trong khi reward tăng — học minimal intermediate responses, reserve effective decision cho final assassination. Merlin/Servant/Minion noisy (success depends on broader mixture).
- Robustness: 4 independent seeds, Werewolf social reading reward +20.2/+17.4/+35.7/+14.8 pp (first 20 vs last 20 updates); response length không systematic increase — gains không phải verbosity.
- Vote steering (Minion): target hit rate DeepSeek-7B +28.7%, Qwen2.5-7B +11.8%, GLM-4-9B +5.8%, Qwen2.5-14B +3.2%, Qwen3-4B +2.1%. Wolf-protection: DeepSeek +36.5%, Qwen2.5-7B +12.2%, others ~3.3–3.4%. Werewolf-team win rate: DeepSeek +48.0%, Qwen2.5-7B +16.5%, Qwen2.5-14B +6.1%, GLM +5.8%, Qwen3-4B +4.1%. Response length heterogeneous (Qwen2.5-7B verbose hơn, DeepSeek moderate) — length alone không giải thích steering.
- Team approval (Avalon): target approval outcome cải thiện TẤT CẢ models: Qwen2.5-7B +21.3%, Qwen2.5-14B +12.5%, Qwen3-4B +10.6%, DeepSeek-7B +16.3%, GLM-4-9B +18.1%; positive follow shifts (others vote đúng hướng sau response); DeepSeek cải thiện dù response ngắn hơn.
- Vote-steering robustness (Qwen2.5-7B, 4 runs): +16.5%/+7.0%/+26.3%/+10.9% — direction replicated, magnitude varies; all runs tăng response length (1 route cho model này).
- Prompt-level ablations (Werewolf good-side win rate): SR+SI cải thiện mọi model: Qwen2.5-7B 24→37% (+13pp), Qwen3-4B 21→34% (+13pp), Qwen2.5-14B 15→23% (+8pp), DeepSeek-7B 14→19% (+5pp), GLM-4-9B 19→22% (+3pp). SR alone cải thiện all; SI alone variable — Qwen3-4B SI-only REDUCE mạnh (12%) — influence planning chỉ beneficial khi grounded trong accurate belief state.
- Full-game multi-agent RFT: 6-player Werewolf win rate 0.25→~1.0, reward 0.15→0.57, reading 0.05→0.70, influence 0.10→0.60+. 9-player: win 0.10→0.95–1.00, reward 0.10→0.60+, influence →0.70, reading above initial (less monotonic). Avalon: win ~0→0.65–0.75, reward 0.30→0.55, reading 0.85–0.90, influence →1.0. Win rate cải thiện CÙNG với intermediate diagnostics dù được evaluate riêng.
- Cross-model generalization: learned good-side policy win 96% (vs Qwen3-4B) đến 72% (vs Kimi-K2.5); ≥90% vs Qwen3.7-Max, Qwen-Plus, GLM-4-9B-Chat, Qwen-Max. SR-F1 0.523–0.643, SI-Follow 0.583–0.725, Wolf-Exile 62.1%–90.8% — behaviours transfer across opponent families, không phải exploit 1 opponent.
- Human evaluation (blinded, 178 ratings, 89/condition): overall value 1.43→2.52 (+1.09); social reading 1.35→2.25 (+0.90); influence & coordination 1.54→2.70 (+1.16); strategic correctness 1.21→2.38 (+1.17); human-like gameplay 1.64→2.00 (+0.36, P=0.067 — KHÔNG significant). Sign-flip permutation P<0.0001 cho 4 dimensions (trừ human-like). Matched pairs: RFT higher 42/74 overall (tie 25, lower 7); influence 45/22/7; strategic 42/30/2; reading 32/34/8. Robust sau khi loại overlap Qwen3.7-Plus-as-wolf (59 pairs): overall +1.09, reading +0.86, influence +1.31, human-like +0.27, strategic +1.14. → RFT improves what agents infer và actions chúng coordinate, CHƯA fully remove model-like linguistic behaviour — strategic competence và linguistic naturalness partially dissociable.

## Actionable Insights
- ĐỪNG optimize win-rate trực tiếp cho social agents — phân tách reward thành social reading (belief inference scored against hidden state) + social influence (message → downstream behaviour change), terminal win làm held-out diagnostic. Ví dụ: multi-agent customer support — reward correct user-intent inference + measured resolution-shift, không phải chỉ ticket closure.
- Influence WITHOUT accurate beliefs là harmful: SI-only prompt cắt Qwen3-4B win rate 21%→12%. Always ground persuasion trong explicit belief state trước. Ví dụ: negotiation agent — infer counterpart's position trước khi craft persuasive message; coordination amplifies wrong suspicions nếu belief sai.
- Evidence tích lũy quan trọng nhất: gains concentrate ở later game states (Werewolf Day-3 F1 0.34→0.75 vs Day-1 0.42→0.53; Avalon M3 0.41→0.80). Design agents aggregate accumulated evidence, không act trên static priors. Ví dụ: meeting agent — revise read of sentiment sau mỗi speaker, không chỉ từ message đầu tiên.

## Assumptions & Limitations
- Điều kiện: games có explicit hidden states, discrete decisions, well-defined team objectives — đo được social computations; rewards cần operationalize intended social state + downstream effect; GRPO phù hợp vì rewards sparse/noisy/trajectory-dependent (no critic needed).
- Limitation paper thừa nhận: real social environments có ambiguous identities, changing objectives, contested definitions of success; agents tương tác với fixed/slowly-changing policies — simultaneous learning bởi nhiều agents có thể tạo non-stationary dynamics, strategic escalation, cyclic behaviour; influence metrics establish messages are FOLLOWED BY behavioural movement nhưng không identify mechanism (evidence, rhetorical clarity, repetition, confidence, hoặc model-specific exploitation); cross-LLM generalization ≠ generalization to humans (humans interpret confidence, deception, uncertainty, commitment differently).
- Limitation CHƯA nói: dual-use risk (influence mechanisms có thể steer choices, exploit biases, produce deceptive communication — paper thảo luận nhưng không mitigation experiment); human eval pool nhỏ (178 ratings from invited evaluators, descriptive pooled estimates); DeepSeek-7B Avalon curve treated as placeholder (reward shaping trade-off evil recall vs good accuracy); không có LLM-as-judge comparison cho human eval; response-length confound không fully separable khỏi steering effect.
