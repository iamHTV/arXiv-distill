# 2609.13890 — Learning How Much to Collaborate: Difficulty-Aware Topology Selection for Multi-Agent Code Generation (Dats)

## Problem
- Multi-agent code systems deploy với MỘT topology cố định tại design time — sai granularity. Hierarchical role-based mesh (HRM) tốn 9.95× tokens của single call ($0.94 vs $0.09/problem, Claude Opus 4 giá $15/M input + $75/M output → effective $36/M).
- Nhưng collaboration benefit KHÔNG uniform: chia 614 problems (300 APPS intro/interview/competition + 164 HumanEval+ + 150 LiveCodeBench post-cutoff) thành difficulty tertiles, gap HRM vs Single = 2.4 điểm pass@1 (easy third), 10.7 (middle), 21.1 (hard). HumanEval+: 5 topologies trong band 4.2 điểm (cheap nhất 1/10 cost); APPS-Competition: 22 điểm. Fixed-topology system misallocate theo 2 hướng: fixed Single mất 21 điểm ở hard tail; fixed HRM tốn 10× budget ở 2/3 easy problems để được 2–10 điểm (easy third: $0.94/problem để move 91.7%→94.1%).

## Contribution
- Định hình topology choice thành per-problem constrained selection: max E[s_π(x)] s.t. E[c_π(x)] ≤ B. Lagrangian relaxation tách theo problem → optimal policy pointwise π_λ(x) = argmax_t [s_t(x) − λc_t]; λ tìm bằng bisection vì expected cost non-increasing theo λ.
- Dats (Difficulty-Aware Topology Selector): predict-then-optimize — predict s_t(x) = Pr[pass | x, τ_t] cho cả 5 topologies từ problem statement alone (KHÔNG execute topology nào tại decision time, KHÔNG gradient vào agents), rồi apply Eq.(2) với calibrated λ. Factorisation này worth 6.03 points over direct five-way classification from identical inputs.
- Predictor = graph network trên TOPOLOGY graph G_T (5 nodes = 5 topologies, edges theo connectivity order Single–Chain–Star–FullMesh, HRM linked tới Star và FullMesh như 2 structures nó composes) — KHÔNG phải graph trên input statement (không có program để encode tại decision time). Worth 1.7 points over flat multi-label head (5 independent sigmoids), với 13.7K params — ÍT hơn flat MLP vì connectivity prior được supply bởi G_T.
- Budget-matched evaluation protocol: cost-aware router là one-parameter family of systems, KHÔNG phải a system; shared λ=0.2 spread realised spend 21.6pp (30.3%–51.9%) vì λ nhân với score scale riêng của mỗi method → reverse 2 comparisons khi equalise spend.

## Method
- 5 topologies: Single (1 call); Chain (3 calls: proposer→critic→reviser, sequential criticism); Star (4 calls: hub decompose 3 subtasks, 3 workers, deterministic concatenation); FullMesh (3 peers 2 rounds, 6 calls); HRM (manager trên 2 leads mỗi lead 2 workers, 5–8 calls). Cost ratio: 1.00/5.10/5.35/8.74/9.95; USD/problem 0.095/0.483/0.507/0.828/0.943; latency 2.6/8.4/26.1/21.7/44.3s. Stable within 3% across backbones (determined by number and structure of calls, không phải model).
- 39 interpretable features trong 6 groups: lexical (5: word length, sentence count, avg word length, code-span fraction, constraint density — đếm "at most"/"exactly"/"for each"); structural (4: worked examples, edge-case discussion, required functions, input–output complexity); complexity (3: nesting hints "for each pair", large numeric bounds, algorithmic keywords); difficulty (1 scalar: 5-point rating của lightweight scorer); semantic (16: leading singular directions của TF-IDF); algorithmic (10: one-hot dominant technique, keyword vote recover manual labels 85.5%).
- Predictor: shared trunk (standardised affine + ReLU) 39→d context z; node init h^0_t = ReLU(W_0[z ∥ e_t] + b_0) với e_t ∈ R^4 (agent count, edge count, diameter, mean degree của topology's own communication graph); message passing L=2 rounds, weights tied across nodes: h^{ℓ+1}_t = ReLU(W_self h^ℓ_t + W_nbr · mean_{u∈N(t)} h^ℓ_u); per-node sigmoid readout ŝ_t = σ(w⊤h^L_t + b). Loss: mean BCE over 5 node readouts (targets per-topology outcomes — problems mọi topology solve dạy cheap suffices; problems none solve dạy cheapest right). Adam lr 1e-3, early stopping trên 10% internal validation, cap 400 epochs, training <30s laptop CPU.
- Selection: ĉ_t = c_t/max c (Single 0.10, HRM 1.0); τ*(x) = argmax_t [ŝ_t(x) − λĉ_t]. Tại λ=0.06, upgrade Single→HRM phải buy ≥5.4 points predicted success. λ calibrated bằng bisection trên 3-fold split của training fold (target B fraction of always-hierarchical cost, tolerance 0.5% hoặc 60 iterations); 5 outer folds → λ = 0.058, 0.052, 0.065, 0.056, 0.061.
- Budget-matched protocol 4 steps: (1) declare B là fraction of realised spend của reference policy không cần calibration (always-hierarchical); (2) calibrate MỖI method riêng bằng bisection trên own coefficient trong training folds, KHÔNG touch test fold; (3) verify realised spend out of fold, tolerance 0.4pp (all 6 methods meet); (4) compare accuracies only within matched group; fixed topologies/oracle report separately như frontier locators. 21.4 bisection steps trung bình; costs no inference trên cached grid. KHÔNG equalise: latency, worst-case cost, variance.
- Setup: 614 problems; 4 backbones (Claude Opus 4 primary, DeepSeek-V3, GPT-4o-mini, Qwen2.5-Coder-32B); temp 0.2, 4096-token cap, pass@1 single sample, program correct chỉ khi pass complete test suite. Full grid 614×5×4 = 12,280 executions, cached, ~$1,807 ($1,753 Opus 4). 5-fold CV stratified on suite × difficulty tertile, 5 seeds. Budget B = 40% always-hierarchical cost = $0.377/problem.
- Baselines: 5 Always-τ; Random; Difficulty-Threshold (bins predicted difficulty → topology, thresholds set on training fold to meet budget); Dats-E2E (five-way softmax on cheapest successful topology — same features/network/calibration); MasRouter-lite (logistic on lexical+structural); RouterR1-lite (ridge regression on accuracy minus normalised cost); RandomForest-Router (300-tree forest in same predict-then-optimize scaffold — strongest learned competitor); Oracle.

## Key Findings
- No topology dominates: aggregate ordering Single 62.21% (382/614) < Chain 69.38% (426) < Star 66.61% (409) < FullMesh 70.52% (433) < HRM 73.62% (452); spread 11.4 points. Chain AHEAD of Star despite 3 vs 4 calls — decomposition có thể split along wrong seam, workers lẫn hub không recover; Chain vs FullMesh indistinguishable (p=0.628) dù FullMesh tốn 71% hơn.
- Dats: 77.69% (477/614) — 25 problems hơn always-hierarchical ở 40% budget, 21 hơn strongest learned competitor (RandomForest 74.27%, 456) cùng budget. +4.07 vs Always-HRM (60% của HRM spend buying nothing); +6.03 vs Dats-E2E; +9.12 vs Random ở 2/3 Random's cost. Oracle 88.93% (546) tại $0.265 — Dats recovers 26.6% distance Always-HRM→oracle. 5 seeds: 77.52±0.71 (margin ~5 seed SD).
- Hard tertile: Dats trên TẤT CẢ 5 fixed topologies (+10.3 points vs HRM); easy third: Dats tracks HRM (mostly declines paying for collaboration).
- Budget matching reverses rankings: shared λ=0.2 → RandomForest 76.55% ($0.489, 51.9%) > RouterR1 75.90% ($0.442) > Dats 75.41% ($0.324, 34.4%) — Dats THIRD; calibrated → Dats 77.69% first, gap 3.42 points over RandomForest. Rank correlation 0.83 nhưng first position flips.
- McNemar: cả 11 comparisons significant sau Holm–Bonferroni; vs Always-HRM: b=60, c=35, χ²=6.06, p_holm=0.0298.
- Routing distribution: 49.0% → Single, 13.0% → HRM, 38% Chain/mid. Suite-dependent: 80% HumanEval+ → Single; 47% APPS-Competition → HRM. Category gradient: implementation/sorting/string 65–73% Single; graph 49% HRM/13% Single; DP 35%/23%; greedy highest Star share 11% (greedy decomposes thành rule + proof of exchange).
- LiveCodeBench: oracle 88.7% vs Dats 68.7% — largest oracle gap; contest statements stylistically uniform → surface features separate poorly dù solutions differ sharply.
- Backbones (retrained each, same 40% budget): gain +4.07 (Opus 4), +4.08 (DeepSeek-V3), +4.23 (GPT-4o-mini), +4.07 (Qwen2.5-Coder-32B) — 4.11±0.07, trong khi single-call accuracy spans 62.21→48.37 (13.8 points). Transfer (Opus-trained router dùng nguyên trên Qwen): 61.89%, +1.79 over always-HRM NHƯNG NOT significant (b=59, c=48, p=0.318) — routing policy transfers, probability calibration shifts → recalibrate on a few hundred cached problems khi đổi backbone.
- Cross-domain math (400 problems: 200 GSM8K + 200 MATH L1–5, exact match, 8 math subject categories replace algorithmic one-hot, keyword vote 82.3%): Dats 88.0% (352/400) vs HRM 84.5% (338), +3.5 points at 40.3% cost ($0.159 vs $0.398/problem), b=25, c=11, χ²=4.69, p=0.030; 5 seeds 87.84±0.86. Gap widens: GSM8K +2.5, MATH L1–2 +3.0, L3 +9.0, L4–5 +20.9 — same shape and magnitude as code (2.4/10.7/21.1). 84% GSM8K single call vs 21% MATH; 29% MATH HRM vs 1.5% GSM8K. Calibrated λ ∈ [0.049, 0.061] overlaps code's [0.052, 0.065] dù cost tables measured independently — λ behaves as price on predicted success.
- Calibration: ECE 0.0275, Brier 0.1723, mild under-confidence ở 0.4–0.7 band (where routing decisions contested).
- Ablations: feature groups removed → Difficulty −4.24, Algorithmic −3.10, Complexity −2.61, Semantic −2.45, Structural −1.31 (ns), Lexical −0.82 (ns) — deployment có thể drop 8/39 features. Architecture: no cost term (λ=0) 83.06% nhưng $0.628 (unconstrained upper bound); fixed λ=0.2 −2.28 và undershoot budget 14%; linear probe −3.75 (interaction difficulty×category không expressible); flat multi-label head −1.66; direct five-way classification −6.03.
- Representation ladder (only encoder changes): Text-GNN (2-layer GCN over statement graph) −1.14 ns; CodeBERT frozen −0.82 ns; GraphCodeBERT frozen −0.33 ns; GraphCodeBERT fine-tuned end-to-end (125M params, 36 min GPU) +0.49 ns; hybrid +1.30 ns — tất cả p>0.35, spread 2.44pp. 125M-param pretrained code knowledge fine-tuned buys half a point KHÔNG distinguish được from noise (seed SD 0.71).
- Router cost: feature extraction <1ms/problem (linear in statement length); forward pass negligible vs 8.4s cheapest topology call; training <30s CPU.

## Actionable Insights
- Route topology per problem, not per system: trên 614 problems, fixed HRM tốn 10× budget để mua 2.4 điểm ở easy third nhưng mua 21.1 điểm ở hard third — router calibrated at 40% HRM cost beats always-HRM 4.07 points. Ví dụ: coding agent với 5 pipeline templates (single/chain/star/mesh/HRM): extract 39 features (difficulty rating + algorithmic category là 2 groups mang nhiều signal nhất, −4.24/−3.10 khi bỏ), train GNN predictor trên cached outcomes, bisection λ to budget → chọn topology trước khi call model nào.
- So sánh cost-aware routers phải báo realised spend bên cạnh accuracy và equalise spend: shared λ=0.2 để 6 methods spend 30.3%–51.9% budget (spread 21.6pp) và reverse rankings (RandomForest first ở shared λ, Dats first khi calibrated, gap 3.42 points). Ví dụ: dashboard đánh giá agent luôn in USD/problem + % of reference budget; calibrate mỗi method bằng bisection trước khi so sánh.
- Hard problems theo category khác nhau: graph problems 49% → HRM, implementation/string 65–73% → Single, greedy → Star (decomposes thành rule + exchange proof). Ví dụ: routing rule of thumb — trước khi chạy multi-agent, classify dominant technique; chỉ graph/DP/hard mới đáng trả 5–10× tokens.
- Predict-then-optimize beats direct classification 6.03 points: label "topology that solved it" ambiguous khi nhiều topologies succeed (38% problems) và undefined khi none do — predict 5 outcome probabilities rồi optimize với cost table (single scalar λ recalibratable WITHOUT retraining khi budget thay đổi). Ví dụ: đổi budget 40%→60% chỉ cần bisection lại λ, KHÔNG retrain.

## Assumptions & Limitations
- Catalogue deliberately nhỏ (5 hand-designed topologies) — claim là giá trị của việc CHỌN topology, không phải giá trị của topology cụ thể.
- LiveCodeBench oracle gap 20 points (88.7% vs 68.7%): contest statements stylistically uniform → surface features fail; router "confused about what the problems are", không phải about what topologies do.
- Transfer across backbones NOT significant (+1.79, p=0.318): phải recalibrate λ (và có thể retrain) trên few hundred cached problems khi đổi backbone.
- Budget-matched protocol equalise expected monetary spend, KHÔNG equalise latency, worst-case cost, hay variance — method meet budget bằng occasionally choosing expensive topology là khác với method never does.
- Math cross-domain: 400 problems, one backbone — establishes mechanism không code-specific, chưa characterize routing for math.
- pass@1 single sample (temp 0.2) — router chỉ giúp dưới repeated sampling là solving different problem (paper không cover).
- Oracle không achievable — bounds catalogue potential; 73.4% oracle gap còn lại (88.93−77.69) chưa claim được.
