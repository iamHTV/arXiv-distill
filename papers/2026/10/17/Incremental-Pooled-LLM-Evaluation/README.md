# 2609.02745 — Incremental Pooled LLM Evaluation for Cost-Effective Retrieval Model Selection

## Problem
- Chọn retrieval model cho production RAG cần reliable comparative evaluation, nhưng relevance judgments at scale expensive và khó repeat khi candidate systems mới arrive. Đánh giá N systems độc lập với LLM judge tốn O(N·Q·K) judgments (queries × top-k docs) với substantial redundancy — different retrieval systems thường surface overlapping document sets.
- Gold standard (pooled human judgments theo TREC) expensive, slow, khó repeat. Classical TREC pooling có pool bias: unjudged documents treated as non-relevant → systems với novel retrieval behavior bị penalise.

## Contribution
- Pooled LLM evaluation: LLM judge đánh giá UNION of documents retrieved bởi tất cả systems under test; judgments reused to compute standard IR metrics cho mọi system trong pool. Incremental: thêm system S_{N+1} bằng cách retrieve + judge CHỈ documents mới nó contributes (R_{N+1}(q)\P(q)); mọi previous judgments reused exactly; new system immediately comparable on equal footing.
- 4 advantages: (1) cost amortization — shared documents judged once, mỗi system thêm chỉ marginal cost cho uniquely retrieved docs; (2) ranking stability — P@k và DCG@k exactly invariant under pool expansion (phụ thuộc own top-k + frozen labels); Recall@k, AP, nDCG@k preserve per-query pairwise orderings (numerator fixed, denominator shared, all systems scaled by same factor); macro-averaged benchmark-level invariance KHÔNG guaranteed in general (per-query scaling factors differ) → tested empirically; (3) reduced pool bias — judge mọi newly retrieved document, KHÔNG systematic penalty cho novel behavior; (4) accuracy — nDCG@10 ρ = 0.69–0.95 vs gold, 74% pairwise disagreements within gold's own bootstrap sampling noise.
- Validation: 4 retrieval benchmarks × 11 systems (dense/sparse/hybrid); production deployment 62 retrieval configurations cho financial news QA system.

## Method
- 4 steps: (1) Retrieve — mỗi system Si, query q → top-k Ri(q); (2) Pool — P(q) = ∪ Ri(q); (3) Judge — mỗi unique (q,d) pair, graded relevance 3-point scale (0 not relevant, 1 partially, 2 highly) từ LLM judge; (4) Evaluate — standard IR metrics cho mỗi system dùng shared pool judgments restricted to Ri(q).
- Datasets: FiQA (648 queries, 57K corpus, 249,865 pool, 1,706 qrels, ~2.6 rel/query, binary); TREC-COVID (50 queries, 171K corpus, 18,870 pool, 66,336 qrels, ~1,327 rel/query, 0–2 scale); NQ (first 500/3,452 test queries, 2.68M corpus, 188,903 pool, 3,452 qrels, ~1.0 rel/query, binary); FinRAG-V (536 queries, 10.9K corpus, 192,783 pool, 606 qrels, ~1.1 rel/query, binary — financial multimodal; layout-aware parser extract text/tables/figures, MLLM generate NL descriptions cho tables/figures, interleave trong reading order, XML tags mark modality provenance).
- Systems: 5 embedding families (OpenAI text-embedding-3-large, Cohere Embed v4, Amazon Titan Embed v2, Nomic embed-text-v1.5, Nomic embed-text-v2-moe) × 3 configurations (dense cosine, sparse BM25, hybrid reciprocal rank fusion) → 5 dense + 5 hybrid + 1 sparse = 11/dataset. Matryoshka truncation to 256 dims where supported.
- Judge: GPT-4.1 (gpt-4.1-2025-04-14), temperature 0.0, single-turn prompt (no in-context examples, no system-level calibration) → reproducible, order-independent. Prompt yêu cầu brief chain-of-thought ("thought" field ≤2 lines) rồi numeric score trong structured JSON; documents presented Title/Content format.
- Judge consistency: re-judge 1,000 (q,d) pairs/dataset — exact 3-point agreement 93–96% (κ=0.84–0.90), ≥99.9% within ±1 grade; binarized (1–2 = relevant) 96–98%.
- Cross-judge: Claude Sonnet 4.6, same prompt, 1,000 pairs/dataset — binarized agreement 84–92% (κ=0.65–0.77); full TREC-COVID pool re-judged (18,870 docs): dù 27% item-level disagreement, system rankings highly correlated (ρ=0.89 nDCG@10, 0.92 R@100, 0.88 MAP), 89% pairwise agreement nDCG@10 và R@100; same top system trên 2 most important metrics; P@10 less stable (sensitivity to small number of judgments per query).
- Protocol: top-100 per query; compute P@k, R@k, nDCG@k, MAP vs pseudolabels (all datasets) và gold qrels (all four; NQ và FinRAG-V qrels very sparse ~1 relevant doc/query).
- Bootstrap: B=10,000 iterations resample queries with replacement, recompute macro-averaged metric, re-rank; swap probability cho pair (A,B) = fraction iterations ordering reverses; pair "qrels-uncertain" nếu swaps >5%; pairwise disagreement classified within qrels noise nếu qrels bản thân uncertain về pair đó.

## Key Findings
- Rank correlation (pooled LLM vs gold qrels, all p<0.05): FiQA nDCG@10 ρ=0.909/τ=0.782, P@10 0.918, R@100 0.864, MAP 0.855; TREC-COVID 0.691/0.564, 0.636, 0.818, 0.736 (lower — gap general-purpose LLM vs specialist biomedical assessors); NQ 0.945/0.818, 0.864, 0.736, 0.718; FinRAG-V 0.909/0.782, 0.936, 0.936, 0.882. Strongest ở FiQA/NQ (ρ≥0.91) nơi LLM's broader relevance notion aligns với sparse qrels.
- Pairwise agreement (55 pairs/dataset): FiQA 89–91%, TREC-COVID 76–78%, NQ 80–91%, FinRAG-V 87–93%.
- Bootstrap: nDCG@10 — 20/27 disagreements (74%) within qrels noise, chỉ 7 genuine ranking differences; all metrics combined — 74/112 (66%) within noise trên 3 BEIR benchmarks; FinRAG-V 4/6 within uncertainty. Most pseudolabel ranks fall within bootstrap 95% rank CIs của qrels. Rank-1 correctly identified trên FiQA across all four metrics; disagreements trên NQ/TREC-COVID chỉ nơi qrels margin giữa top-2 negligible (bootstrap swap probability >40%). 97% pairwise orderings preserved once bootstrap uncertainty in qrels taken into account.
- Cost amortization: FiQA 712,800→249,865 judgments (65% saved); TREC-COVID 550,000→188,903 (66%); NQ 589,600→192,783 (67%); FinRAG-V 55,000→18,870 (66%). Total 1,907,400→650,421 — pooling eliminates ~1.26M redundant judgments (66%).
- Ranking stability under 500 random system-addition orderings (pool grown 1→11 systems): NQ và TREC-COVID ZERO reversals; FiQA 53.8% orderings trigger reversal nhưng confined to single pair differing 0.000014 nDCG@10 (dense-nomic-1.5 vs hybrid-titan-v2) + second pair 2.4%; FinRAG-V 1.4% (dense-emb3-large vs dense-titan-v2, Δ=0.0006). No pair with meaningful gap (Δ>0.001) reverses under any ordering on any dataset — fragile pairs chính là pairs bootstrap CIs identify statistically indistinguishable.
- Calibration bias: pseudolabel scores differ absolute magnitude — LLM broader relevance inflates nDCG (+0.1% đến +51%) và deflates recall (−17% đến −39%), nhưng approximately uniform across systems (within-column std ≤12.8pp nDCG, ≤11.1pp recall) → preserves ordinal rankings.
- Pool bias (leave-one-family-out, 6 families): classical pooling FiQA 8 swaps (max penalty 0.033), TREC-COVID 10 swaps (0.055), FinRAG-V 1 swap (0.012), NQ 0 swaps (0.008). Pooled LLM judging newly contributed documents removed ALL 19 pairwise ranking errors classical pooling introduced trên 3 affected datasets.
- Production deployment: ~300k news article pages trong OpenSearch (1 month licensed data); 303 queries từ 4 sources (LLM-generated từ semantic clusters 135, metadata-clustered 91, templatic latest-news 50, real production API traffic 27) + perturbations (keyword-reduced, instruction-framed). 62 configurations: 5 embedding models (emb3-large, Cohere v4, Nomic v1.5, Nomic v2-moe, MiniLM-L12-v2) × 3 modes × 2–3 dimensionalities (256/768/1536) × 3 query formulations + query expansion variants. Final pool 766,350 unique judgments (GPT-4.1) vs 3,765,643 independent → 79.6% reuse, 4.9× cost reduction; pool built incrementally over 4 weeks, no point nào cần re-run previous judgments. Cost ≈ $800 (GPT-4.1 $2/M input, $8/M output).
- Production rankings: top-5 MAP@100 all hybrid — (1) hybrid-emb3-large-768 (0.461), (2) hybrid-emb3-large-256 (0.459), (3) hybrid-nomic-1.5-768 (0.454), (4) hybrid-nomic-v2-moe-768 (0.454), (5) hybrid-cohere-v4-1536 (0.454). Selected hybrid-emb3-large-256 (results + latency + embedding cost).
- Findings: higher dimensions improve dense (ΔMAP +0.029 cho 256→768/1536) nhưng gap narrows under hybrid (+0.012) — BM25 fusion compensates reduced embedding capacity. Hybrid robust hơn với query formulation: keyword-reduced hurt dense hơn (ΔMAP −0.013); instruction-framed degrade ALL systems (ΔMAP −0.039) by diluting retrieval signal. Query expansion KHÔNG help well-formed queries (MAP 0.424 vs 0.459) nhưng robustness mechanism against verbose formulations — nearly eliminating framing-induced recall@100 penalty (−4.8pp → −1.1pp).
- Incremental pool growth works: began 5 systems, grew to 62 over 4 weeks; early conclusions ("hybrid > dense") không bị invalidated bởi later additions. Same pool answered unplanned questions (dimensionality under hybrid, keyword queries harder, query expansion on framed queries) — zero additional LLM cost.

## Actionable Insights
- Bắt đầu benchmark với 3–5 systems, grow incrementally: mỗi model mới chỉ judge uniquely retrieved documents (65–80% reuse). Ví dụ: RAG system đánh giá embedding model mới — retrieve top-100, judge chỉ docs chưa có trong pool, so sánh ngay với all previous systems on equal footing.
- Pooled LLM judging tránh classical pool bias: judge mọi newly contributed document → removed all 19 ranking errors classical pooling introduced. Ví dụ: KHÔNG dùng TREC pooling unjudged-as-nonrelevant khi so sánh hybrid vs dense (novel behavior bị penalise 0.033–0.055 nDCG@10).
- Bootstrap qrels trước khi tin pairwise disagreement: 74% disagreements nằm trong gold's own sampling noise; chỉ 7/220 pairs genuine. Ví dụ: dashboard so sánh 2 retrieval models — compute bootstrap swap probability (10,000 resamples); nếu >5% thì pair "effectively tied", đừng quyết định model dựa trên cặp đó.
- Hybrid retrieval là default cho production RAG: top-5 all hybrid, robust hơn với query formulation (keyword/framed degrade dense nhiều hơn); BM25 fusion compensates low dimensionality → dùng 256-dim embeddings (Matryoshka truncation) + BM25 thay vì 1536-dim dense — latency + embedding cost thấp hơn, MAP tương đương (0.459 vs 0.461).
- KHÔNG dùng instruction-framed queries cho retrieval evaluation (degrade ALL systems ΔMAP −0.039 by diluting retrieval signal); KHÔNG dùng query expansion cho well-formed queries (0.424 vs 0.459) — chỉ dùng expansion như robustness mechanism cho verbose formulations (−4.8pp → −1.1pp recall penalty).
- Pin judge model version + temperature 0 để preclude drift within evaluation campaign; nếu judge updated → selective re-judging sample validates continuity. Ví dụ: log judge version trong metadata mỗi evaluation run; re-judge 1,000 pairs khi upgrade GPT-4.1 → GPT-5.

## Assumptions & Limitations
- Tight clusters: systems cách nhau <0.001 nDCG (e.g. NQ top-2) — neither qrels nor pseudolabels distinguish được (bootstrap swap >40% cả hai label sources); method designed for comparative selection, KHÔNG resolving effectively tied systems.
- Limited judge diversity: rankings từ single primary judge (GPT-4.1); Sonnet 4.6 validation chỉ full re-judge trên TREC-COVID; correlation/bias characteristics có thể differ cho smaller, open-weight, hoặc domain-specialized judges. Frozen-model assumption: nếu provider deprecates pinned checkpoint → cần re-judge strategy.
- Calibration bias uniform nhưng non-zero: pseudolabel nDCG inflated +0.1%–51%, recall deflated −17%–39% → absolute scores KHÔNG comparable với human-scale metrics, chỉ ordinal rankings đáng tin.
- Macro-averaged benchmark-level invariance KHÔNG guaranteed theoretically (per-query scaling factors differ under pool expansion) — empirically stable trong 500 orderings nhưng fragile pairs (Δ<0.001) có thể reverse.
- Judge consistency measured 93–96% exact (κ=0.84–0.90) — 4–7% of judgments not reproducible exactly; suitable for reuse but not for per-document certification.
- FinRAG-V qrels very sparse (~1 relevant doc/query) — correlation strong (ρ=0.91) nhưng sparse qrels hạn chế measurement precision.
- Production corpus ~300k news pages, 1 month, licensed — generalization sang domains khác (biomedical TREC-COVID lower correlation 0.64–0.82) chưa established.
