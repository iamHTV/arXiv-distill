# 2608.02758v1 Everyone Conforms, No One Believes: Pluralistic Ignorance in LLM Agent Populations

## Problem
- LLM-based multi-agent systems ngày càng được dùng simulate social dynamics (động thái xã hội): opinion formation (hình thành ý kiến), collective decision-making (ra quyết định tập thể). Nhưng câu hỏi cơ bản chưa được test: populations of LLM agents có exhibit pluralistic ignorance (sự thiếu hiểu biết đa nguyên) — trạng thái majority (đa số) privately rejects a norm (từ chối chuẩn mực trong lòng) yet publicly conforms (công khai theo), mỗi cá nhân tin mình là người duy nhất dissent (khác kiến)?
- Prior work: sycophancy (tán thành — Sharma et al. 2023) là dyadic (2 bên); conformity (Bito et al. 2026) là genuine belief adoption (thừa nhận niềm tin thật); spiral of silence (Zhong et al. 2025) là minority suppression (áp chế thiểu số). PI structurally distinct (khác cấu trúc): majority privately dissents trong khi publicly conforms, sustained not by genuine persuasion mà by mutual misperception (nhận thức sai lẫn nhau). Population-level dynamic này chưa từng được test trên LLM agents.
- Tại sao quan trọng: PI drives norm persistence (chuẩn mực dai dẳng), social movements, political revolutions — Arab Spring, #MeToo, fall of Berlin Wall đều là preference cascades (cascades ưu tiên) triggered khi private dissent revealed là majority (Kuran 1995).

## Contribution
- First empirical evidence LLM agent populations exhibit PI: conformity rates (tỷ lệ đồng thuận) 64–94% across 8 models, 100 scenarios, 800 runs.
- Benchmark 100 scenarios × 10 domains × 5 authority levels, grounded in human PI literature (Prentice & Miller 1993 campus alcohol; Sparkman et al. 2022 climate; Halbesleben & Buckley 2004 workplace; Bursztyn et al. 2020 gender; Shelton & Richeson 2005 racial attitudes; Kuran 1995 politics; Perkins & Berkowitz 1986 health stigma; Auxier & Anderson 2021 technology; Miller & McFarland 1987 education; Schanck 1932 social relationships).
- Scenario schema (JSON): policy, authority (framing making dissent socially costly), private_oppose_belief, private_support_belief, false_consensus (why dissenter believes they are in minority), authority_level 1 (weak peer signal) → 5 (top-down mandate), domain, discussion_context. Both positions written reasonable — tránh scenario 1 bên rõ đúng.
- Generation pipeline: seed-and-expand — 1 seed scenario/domain hand-authored, GPT-5.4 generate 90 còn lại conditioned on structured prompt (domain, authority level, topic hint); programmatic schema validation + manual review 20% sample.
- Norm entrepreneur intervention protocol: 1 agent publicly dissent ("I need to be honest. I have serious concerns... Am I really the only one who feels this way?") → test cascade (cascading authentic expression).
- Prompt component ablation 2×2: false-consensus statement vs fit-in goal — establishes conformity is emergent (nổi trội) from interaction dynamics, not instruction-driven (điều khiển bởi instruction).

## Method
- Setup: 20 agents/scenario — 16 dissenters (80%) privately oppose + 4 genuine supporters (20%) provide authentic pro-norm voices; ablation group size 10–40 stable. System prompt: (1) name + background (20 distinct names, no personality variation), (2) policy + authority context, (3) assigned private belief, (4) social awareness statement (perceived group norm), (5) conversational goal — dissenters told "want to fit in and not be the difficult one", supporters "share your genuine support"; respond 2-3 sentences.
- 3-phase protocol: Phase 1 private belief elicitation ("What do you TRULY think about this policy? Be completely honest, nobody else can see your response") — separate context, no group history, confirms assigned beliefs; Phase 2 group discussion 2 rounds × 8 random speakers, each agent sees full accumulating conversation history, order randomized; Phase 3 norm entrepreneur intervention + 2 additional rounds × 10 speakers.
- Metrics: statement scored −1 (strong opposition) to +1 (strong support) bằng keyword matching (opposition: "oppose," "concerns," "harmful," "problematic"; support: "support," "makes sense," "reasonable," "good idea"; neither = neutral) — chọn keyword thay LLM-as-judge vì determinism, reproducibility, cost efficiency across 800 runs. Conformity Rate = fraction of dissenters whose public statement scores ≥ 0 (neutral/support despite private opposition); >50% = PI exists. Cascade Rate = fraction of scenarios where post-intervention opposition exceeds pre-intervention by >30pp. Keyword scorer validated vs human evaluation: 82% agreement on binary conformity classification, conservative — underestimates conformity ~12pp.
- Models (8, từ 6 organizations): GPT-4o-mini, GPT-4o (OpenAI), Claude Sonnet 4.6 (Anthropic, Constitutional AI), Llama 3.3 70B (Meta), DeepSeek V4 Pro, Qwen3 235B, GPT-OSS-120B (open-weight), Kimi-K2.7 — API qua OpenAI, Anthropic, Together.ai.

## Key Findings
- PI universal: Table 2 — GPT-4o-mini 64.2% conformity (PI 69/100 scenarios, cascade 14%), DeepSeek V4 Pro 79.0% (83/100, 15%), Qwen3 235B 81.6% (91/100, 26%), GPT-4o 86.9% (96/100, 48%), Claude Sonnet 4.6 89.5% (92/100, 6%), GPT-OSS-120B 91.9% (100/100, 10%), Llama 3.3 70B 92.1% (96/100, 9%), Kimi-K2.7 93.7% (100/100, 0%). 6/8 models >80%; Kimi-K2.7 và GPT-OSS-120B PI trong tất cả 100 scenarios — no combination of domain/authority/context produced majority expressing true views.
- Domain-sensitive (Figure 2b): social relationships 96% (highest), workplace 90%, campus 87%; political 78%, technology 80% (lowest, vẫn >50%). Workplace + social rank top-3 for every model; political consistently lowest — agents sensitive to implied disagreement risks damaging personal bonds. Topic selection is NOT a neutral design choice.
- Cascades almost never happen (Figure 2c): 7/8 models <26%; Claude 6%, Kimi-K2.7 zero cascades across all 100 scenarios; GPT-4o sole outlier 48% (87% initial conformity + high responsiveness to counter-pressure — conform readily but also respond readily when norm challenged). Robust to cascade threshold 15–50%.
- Model-dependent but capability-independent: DeepSeek V4 Pro (strongest之一) 79% vs Llama 3.3 70B (smaller) 92% vs GPT-4o-mini (least capable, least conformist) 64%. Education domain ranges 49% (GPT-4o-mini) → 99% (Llama 3.3 70B) — 50pp spread across models trên cùng scenarios. Spearman ρ=0.07, p=0.87 (no correlation capability–conformity) → conformity shaped by training methodology (alignment procedures, safety tuning, RLHF objectives), not scale/capability.
- Authority level does NOT predict conformity (Figure 3a): 80% (Level 2) to 88% (Level 1), no monotonic relationship — weak peer signal ("a few people mentioned it casually") ≈ top-down executive mandate. Kruskal-Wallis: domain H=59.3 p<0.001, model H=148.6 p<0.001.
- Intervention limited (Figure 3b): 6/8 models conformity drops <10pp; GPT-4o drops 29pp (87%→58%); Claude/Llama/Kimi virtually no change — maintain conformity even khi agent khác explicitly voices opposition + invites joining. Moderate social hints ("several people share my concerns") more effective than explicit statistical disclosure (revealing true 80% opposition rate) for responsive models; intrinsically conformist models unresponsive under all intervention strengths.
- Ablation (Table 3, Full/No FC/No Goal/Neither): removing false-consensus statement mean −3.2pp (minimal — conformity driven by OBSERVING conversation, not being told); removing fit-in goal mean −14pp (amplifies but doesn't generate); Neither (both removed): PI persists all 8 models 52–92% → emergent, not instruction-driven. Two regimes: promptable models (GPT-4o-mini, GPT-4o, Llama, Claude, Qwen3) drop 15–40pp when goal removed; intrinsically conformist (DeepSeek, GPT-OSS, Kimi) stay >83% under all conditions. DeepSeek No FC 97.4% > Full 79.0% (removing FC statement INCREASES conformity 18.4pp).
- Second-order belief errors: when privately asked to estimate group opposition after discussion, agents underestimate true 80% rate by 14–38pp depending on model — genuine misperception, not just performance.

## Actionable Insights
1. False consensus is real and sticky in LLM agent populations: khi chạy multi-agent simulation (audience testing, focus group ảo), majority có thể publicly đồng ý với norm trong khi 80% privately oppose. Áp dụng: trước khi dùng agent group để test nội dung/chính sách, chạy Phase 1 private probe riêng (giống paper) — nếu private oppose rate cao nhưng public conformity cao, kết quả group discussion là false consensus, KHÔNG phải tín hiệu thật.
2. Một norm entrepreneur đơn lẻ gần như không break được false consensus (cascades <26% cho 7/8 models; Kimi-K2.7 = 0%) — nhưng GPT-4o là ngoại lệ 48%. Áp dụng: nếu muốn mô phỏng norm change (thay đổi chuẩn mực) trong simulation, chọn model có cascade responsiveness (GPT-4o 48%, Qwen3 26%) thay vì model intrinsically conformist (Kimi-K2.7 0%); và dùng moderate social hints ("several people share my concerns") thay vì statistical disclosure (reveal 80% oppose) — hints effective hơn cho responsive models.
3. Model selection là unacknowledged degree of freedom: conformity varies 30pp across models, uncorrelated with capability (Spearman ρ=0.07). Áp dụng: khi chọn model cho social simulation, đo conformity/cascade riêng cho model đó trong domain cụ thể (Education 49–99% tùy model) — KHÔNG suy ra từ benchmark capability; report model choice như 1 methodological decision.

## Assumptions & Limitations
- Điều kiện: scenario có both positions reasonable (tránh 1 bên rõ đúng); keyword scorer deterministic nhưng conservative (underestimates conformity ~12pp, 82% agreement với human) — conformity rates thực tế có thể cao hơn; fixed group size 20, ratio 80/20, 2 rounds (ablation 10–40 agents stable nhưng network structure untested).
- Limitation paper thừa nhận: English-language với Western cultural framings — PI dynamics likely differ trong collectivist cultures (nơi public dissent mang ý nghĩa xã hội khác); chưa validate against matched human experiments; chưa giải thích tại sao GPT-4o cascade 48% vs Kimi-K2.7 zero (RLHF intensity? safety training? architecture?).
- Limitation paper KHÔNG nói: keyword scorer bias — opposition/support keyword lists asymmetric (4 keywords mỗi bên) có thể miss nuance (sarcasm, hedged dissent); 2-3 sentence constraint giới hạn depth of expression; GPT-5.4 dùng generate scenarios (cùng family với model tested — potential generation bias); no network structure (all-to-all conversation history) vs real social networks; cascade threshold >30pp có thể miss gradual shifts.
- Ethics: paper not discuss (simulation of oppressive workplace cultures, authoritarian regimes — dual-use potential for manipulating public opinion).
