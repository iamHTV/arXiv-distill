# 2608.06865v1 Multi-Agent Forensic Reasoning for Generalizable Deepfake Video Detection (ARGUS)

## Problem
- Generative AI mới (Seedance 2.0, Sora 2, Kling...) synth entire faces (toàn bộ khuôn mặt) với stable identity + plausible motion (chuyển động hợp lý) → detectors (bộ phát hiện) trained on earlier techniques suffer substantial degradation trên unseen methods (phương pháp chưa thấy).
- Conventional detectors (small vision models) learn từ binary real-or-fake labels (nhị phân thật/giả) → rely on shortcut features (đặc trưng tắt) specific to datasets/generators → fail trên forgery methods, identities, compression conditions, data sources mới.
- Existing benchmarks: limited coverage of synthesis landscape + only binary labels without visual evidence (bằng chứng hình ảnh) → coarse-grained supervision (giám sát thô) làm khó học subtle cues (dấu vết tinh tế) và khó biết detector đã học transferable forensic knowledge (kiến thức pháp y chuyển giao được) hay chỉ fit dataset biases (thiên kiến tập dữ liệu). Authenticity decisions (quyết định xác thực) without supporting evidence limit trustworthiness và auditability (khả năng kiểm toán) trong high-risk settings.
- Single MLLM (multimodal LLM — LLM đa phương thức) unreliable: overemphasize most salient appearance cue (đặc trưng nổi bật nhất), overlook weak evidence spread across frames, hoặc generate plausible explanation inconsistent với final verdict. Holistic judgment (phán đoán toàn diện) bỏ qua distinct causes của different forgery traces: texture over-smoothing (làm mượt kết cấu), inconsistent illumination (chiếu sáng không nhất quán), unstable motion, physical plausibility violations (vi phạm tính hợp lý vật lý) — mỗi loại cần forensic knowledge khác nhau.

## Contribution
- FaceVid-Forensics-100K: 100,000 videos (21,075 real + 78,925 fake), 33 synthesis methods (phương pháp tổng hợp) across face swapping (FS — đổi mặt), face reenactment (FR — điều khiển biểu diễn lại), entire-face synthesis (EFS — tổng hợp toàn mặt: T2V, I2V, TI2V, V2V), bao gồm recent generators như Seedance 2.0. So với prior datasets (DF40 23 methods, Celeb-DF++ 22, DeepfakeDetection 5): lớn nhất + duy nhất có fine-grained textual labels (Observations + Explanations).
- Fine-grained textual supervision 4 dimensions (kích thước): texture, lighting, motion, physics — observations (quan sát) + verdict-consistent explanations (giải thích nhất quán với phán quyết), auto-synthesized qua multi-model aggregation (tổng hợp đa mô hình) + conflict-resolution (giải quyết xung đột) pipeline.
- Multi-agent forensic reasoning framework: 4 specialized domain-expert agents (texture, lighting, motion, physics) independently analyze forgery cues (dấu vết giả mạo); judge agent (thẩm phán) reconciles reports → final prediction + explanation.
- OOD (out-of-distribution — ngoài phân phối) evaluation: toàn bộ framework là small open-source MLLMs (Qwen2.5-VL-7B base) nhưng outperforms tất cả methods bao gồm closed-source GPT và Gemini, ranks first across all reported metrics.

## Method
- Dataset collection: AIGVDBench (~442,000), GenVidBench (~6,780,000), ViF-Bench (~3,000), FF++ (~5,000) + 2,901 crawled Seedance 2.0/recent (577 retained sau manual screening). Face detection pipeline lọc frame-by-frame → 1,158,585 valid face-centric videos (30,240 real + 1,128,345 fake, 436.56 hours). Dedup real by YouTube ID → 21,075. Hard-sample prioritization: 4 dominant generators (ModelScope, Pika, Text2Video-Zero, VideoCrafter2) evaluated bằng AltFreezing detector, prioritize retaining low scores (harder to distinguish): 235,007→11,958 / 75,471→15,163 / 435,210→10,432 / 376,471→32,690 → fake total 78,925.
- Splits: train ~68.9K, in-domain ~23.5K, OOD ~7.6K (5,716 real + 1,920 fake từ 20 held-out generators: CogVideoX1.5, HunyuanVideo, LTX-Video, SkyReels, Wan, Hailuo, Kling, Pika 2.2, PixVerse, Sora 2, Seedance 2.0...). Generator-disjoint split (tách rời theo generator) — đánh giá generalization đến unseen synthesis methods.
- Label generation: 5 MLLM annotators (GPT-4o, Gemini 3.5-Flash closed-source; Qwen2.5-VL open-source; Skyra, VideoVeritas forensics-tuned) independently generate observations + verdicts; DeepSeek-V4 Pro aggregator (pure text LLM — label aggregation operates trên text reports + ground-truth metadata, không cần multimodal input; decoupled from annotator pool → architectural independence, không inherit inductive biases). Observation labels: merge observations separately per dimension, guided by ground-truth label to resolve cross-model contradictions + filter noise, KHÔNG reveal verdict. Explanation labels: khi có correct predictions → prioritize synthesize explanations từ correct annotators; nếu all wrong → reverse inference (suy luận ngược) — re-evaluate merged observations against ground-truth label.
- Framework: uniformly sample frames X(v) → 4 observation agents, mỗi agent nhận same frames + dimension-specific prompt → textual observation KHÔNG predict verdict (texture agent: skin details, blending boundaries; lighting: illumination, shadow consistency; motion: temporal instability, flicker; physics: anatomical/geometric plausibility). Judge agent receives 4 textual observations + optionally video frames → weigh supporting/conflicting evidence → binary real/fake + concise verdict-consistent explanation.
- Training: 2 stages. (1) SFT: observation agents trained independently on dimension-specific observation labels (autoregressive LM loss); judge trained on concatenated observations + visual input. (2) GRPO (Group Relative Policy Optimization — tối ưu hóa chính sách tương đối nhóm): judge only, observation agents frozen; G=8 candidate responses per prompt, binary accuracy reward r∈{0,1}, advantage A = (r − r̄)/(σ_r + ε) group-normalized, clipped surrogate objective + KL-divergence penalty vs frozen SFT policy (giữ explanation quality).
- Implementation: Qwen2.5-VL-7B base cho tất cả agents (fair comparison với Skyra); ms-swift; LoRA + AdamW; LR 1×10⁻⁴ (SFT), 5×10⁻⁶ (GRPO); 1 epoch; thinking mode disabled khi inference.

## Key Findings
- OOD test set (Table 2): Ours (w/ Video) Acc 69.87%, Recall 81.82%, F1 53.28% — best across all metrics; Ours (w/o Video) 67.41/65.00/51.01 — second best. Strongest single-model baseline Gemini-2.5-Pro F1 47.45% → framework improves F1 +5.83pp absolute. Judge access video trực tiếp: F1 51.01→53.28 (original visual information supplements expert reports).
- So sánh: small vision models (DFGaze 56.67/17.92/27.24; DFD-FCG 61.25/29.22/39.16; Effort 63.92/34.79/44.76; TALL++ 63.47/42.97/45.07; TFCU 64.28/33.44/45.20); open-source MLLMs (Qwen2.5-VL-7B 35.94/16.51/13.24; InternVL3.5-8B 37.87/17.60/14.53; MiMo-V2.5 49.85/15.00/18.69; Qwen3.6-35B-A3B 53.17/50.05/35.72); closed-source (GPT-4o 57.63/40.36/37.55; GPT-5-mini 59.31/38.70/39.00; Gemini-2.5-Pro 63.78/75.29/47.45; Gemini-3.5-Flash 63.34/58.75/46.22); forensics-tuned (Skyra 60.50/78.96/38.30; VideoVeritas 57.87/65.00/43.22).
- Ablation per agent (Table 3, all-four w/o Video 63.61/82.24/47.53): texture-only best single agent (58.39/97.40/44.53 w/o; 66.90/87.86/50.39 w/) — texture cues (kết cấu) mạnh nhất; lighting weakest single (54.70/75.31/40.54). Gap giữa single-agent và all-four rõ hơn khi KHÔNG có video → 4 perspectives provide distinct evidence; khi có video, judge recovers part of missing visual info → gap hẹp lại.
- Training stages (Table 4): Training-Free 45.06/65.05/33.53 (w/o Video); +SFT Only-Obs 50.63/99.58/40.46 (recall gần 100% nhưng precision thấp); +SFT Only-Judge 51.42/99.95/40.87; +SFT Joint 63.61/82.24/47.53; +SFT+GRPO 67.41/65.00/51.01 (w/o Video) — jointly training both components better than either alone; GRPO thêm gain.
- Reasoning strategies (Table 5, training-free): multi-turn variants (CoT, Multi-turn, Multi-turn-Obs, Multi-turn-All) KHÔNG improve over Single trên Qwen2.5-VL-7B (F1 13.24 → 9.50–11.92, worse!) → improvement đến từ independent evidence collection + judge reconciliation, KHÔNG phải longer prompts, additional dialogue turns, hoặc repeated frame access. InternVL3.5-8B: multi-turn giúp (F1 14.53→25.85) nhưng framework vẫn best (29.81/30.52).

## Actionable Insights
1. Phân rã forensic analysis thành 4 independent perspectives (texture/lighting/motion/physics) + judge reconcile mạnh hơn single holistic judgment: F1 47.45% (best single) → 53.28% (+5.83pp) trên OOD generators unseen trong training. Áp dụng: khi cần verify authenticity nội dung (video marketing, UGC — user-generated content), chạy 4 agent kiểm tra riêng 4 khía cạnh rồi summary judge — rẻ hơn (small open-source models) và chính xác hơn closed-source GPT/Gemini single-model.
2. Texture cues là dấu hiệu mạnh nhất (texture-only F1 44.53, recall 97.40%): skin over-smoothing, blending boundaries, plastic-like finger textures. Áp dụng: khi review video suspect, zoom vào skin/edges/hands trước — texture anomalies (bất thường kết cấu) là nơi deepfake mới nhất vẫn lộ.
3. Multi-turn prompting KHÔNG giúp (thậm chí hại trên Qwen2.5-VL-7B: CoT F1 11.29 < Single 13.24) — improvement đến từ specialization + reconciliation, không phải từ longer reasoning chains. Áp dụng: đừng waste tokens CoT/multi-turn trên single MLLM cho deepfake detection; thay vào đó chia nhỏ task cho specialized agents.

## Assumptions & Limitations
- Điều kiện: framework cần face-centric videos (face detection pipeline lọc frames không có mặt); OOD split chỉ chứa EFS (entire-face synthesis) generators — FS/FR generalization chưa test trên held-out methods; base model Qwen2.5-VL-7B (all agents same backbone).
- Limitation paper thừa nhận (implicit): recall-precision tradeoff — Only-Obs recall 99.58% nhưng F1 40.46 (precision rất thấp); GRPO cải thiện decision policy nhưng giữ observation agents frozen (observation quality capped bởi SFT).
- Limitation paper KHÔNG nói: dataset labels do MLLM annotators + aggregator tạo (không human-verified — potential annotator bias/hallucination trong supervision); ground-truth-guided conflict resolution có thể circular (annotators thấy video, aggregator biết label → labels có thể leak label-specific artifacts); OOD recall 81.82% nghĩa 18% fake videos missed; precision không report trực tiếp (Acc 69.87% với 5,716 real + 1,920 fake → nhiều real misclassified); efficiency/latency của 5-agent pipeline không benchmarked (4 agents + judge chạy sequential = ~5× inference cost); thinking mode disabled có thể underestimate MLLM baselines.
- Ethics: dual-use risk (deepfake detectors có thể bị dùng ngược để cải thiện generators); dataset chứa crawled internet videos.
